Article Heading

An article is a self-contained piece of text, for example a blog post or a news report. It typically has its own heading, and possibly also subheadings.

An article should also be understandable independently of the HTML page in which it appears.

Sidebar

The sidebar contains things that are only peripherally related to the main topic, for example comments, references, or trivia.

Footer, for example with copyright information.
